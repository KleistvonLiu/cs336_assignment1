Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max liked to play with his ball. One day, Max saw a big mixer in the kitchen. Max thought it would be fun to play with the mixer.
Max went to the kitchen and found a lot of yummy food. He started to eat, but then something unexpected happened. Max found a big, yummy fish. Max ate the fish, and suddenly, the fish jumped out of the water! Max was so surprised and happy.
Max's friends helped him get the fish back. They all played with the fish and the fish all day. Max, his friends, and Max had the best time ever. And Max was never hungry again.
